# RUIN Pathfinder

Questing routes, one step at a time, with a waypoint arrow, Questie's quest database when you have it, which RUIN members share your quests, and routes written and shared by RUIN's own route authors.

Part of the **RUIN Gaming Companion App**. Requires **RUIN Core** (0.3.14 or later). Works with **Questie** when installed: Pathfinder reads Questie's quest database while you play (nothing of Questie's is copied into RUIN) for where quests start and end, where their targets are, their level and objectives, and the quests available in your zone. Route steps carry quest IDs, so this works for every built-in, recorded and shared route.

## The experience
- **Where it lives:** the Pathfinder tab in the RUIN window (Guide, Quests, Routes, Record), the on-screen tracker, a pin on the map, `/path`, and key bindings for next and previous step.
- **Checklist:** the route's steps tick off as you go, each with a marker in RUIN's colors (yellow quest, blue turn-in, purple class quest and trainer, blue waypoint) and where it happens; a legend sits beside the list. When a step with a place begins, chat says "Waypoint set: <quest> (252 yards)".
- **Class track:** your class and the next trainer visit, with a reminder at each new even level (`/path trainer` turns it off). That rule is Classic's and is marked unverified for WoW Forever until RUIN confirms it.
- **Following a route:** pick a route and press *Start*. Accept, finish and turn-in steps tick off by themselves (by quest ID); the tracker glows when the step changes. With *Skip done quests* on (`/path skip`), steps for quests you've already done, or already have, tick themselves off too; going back with < pauses that until you pass the step again.
- **Questie:** steps without a place get one from Questie (the giver, the hand-in, or the nearest spot of the targets), step tooltips show the quest's level, giver, hand-in and objectives, and *Draft from Questie* (`/path draft`) makes a route of the quests Questie lists for your zone and level.
- **Quests page:** your quest log with Questie's details and, for each quest, who in RUIN has it, who is ready to hand it in and who has done it. *Show on map* marks where to go next; *Invite them* invites the members who have it. The Guide marks steps others are on ("2 in RUIN"), and *RUIN on the road* counts the quests you have in common.
- **Waypoint arrow:** steps with a place show an arrow and the distance on the tracker, and a pin on the world map and minimap. In another zone the tracker names the zone instead. Toggle with *Arrow + pin* or `/path arrow`.
- **RUIN routes from officers:** officers press *Publish* on any route (or *Approve* on one a member shared) and it lands in every RUIN member's route list with an officer badge. Hover the badge to see who made or approved it; officers can *Withdraw* it again.
- **Leveling together:** the tracker counts RUIN members on your step, and the Guide page lists everyone on a route. Press *Follow* next to a partner and your tracker moves when theirs does.
- **Record:** route authors press *Start recording* and play. Every quest you accept, finish and hand in becomes a step with its place, and level-ups, gear you receive (with its item ID) and the minutes between steps are written down too. A "Recording: ON" badge shows while it runs. Add go-to, note, class quest and trainer steps with the box, or type `/path mark <note>` where you stand; `/path rec` shows the status. *Save route* adds it to your routes. This is how RUIN turns beta play into real WoW Forever routes and gear times.
- **Sharing:** *Export* gives a copy-paste string (RUIN-ROUTE1:...), *Import string* reads one, and *Share* sends a route to everyone in RUIN, who find it on their Routes page to add.
- **Commands:** `/path next`, `/path prev`, `/path tracker`, `/path arrow`, `/path skip`, `/path quests`, `/path draft`, `/path questshare`, `/path record`, `/path rec`, `/path mark <note>`, `/path trainer`, `/path save`, `/path import`, `/path unfollow`.

## What it shares with RUIN
The route you're on and your step number (when it changes and every 2 minutes); the quest IDs in your log, which are ready to hand in, and which of your route's quests you've done (when they change, at most every 20 seconds, and every 5 minutes; *Share with RUIN* on the Quests page or `/path questshare` turns this off); and routes you choose to share.

## Notes
Built-in Alliance routes cover levels 1–30 (Elwynn, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil, Zephras Isle, Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore, Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale and the Hall of Thanes), checked against the WoW Forever quest database (beta 1.60.1: Wowhead Forever, wowforeverquests.com, foreverchanges.pro and wowforever.ninja) and cross-checked with QuestieDB's Forever data. Givers and hand-ins sit where the Forever database puts them; quest locations for Forever's new quests are still being mapped, and coordinates from the Classic baseline can be off where Forever moved things. The two original samples (Valley of Trials, Northshire) use WoW Classic quest names and are labelled as samples. Recorded times stay on your account; shared and exported routes carry the steps only. Recorded routes use the places you actually stood.
